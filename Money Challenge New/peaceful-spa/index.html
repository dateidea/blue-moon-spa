First time? Read this.

A few quick answers.

Should I call before visiting?

It helps a lot, especially on evenings and weekends. We can hold a room and let you know exactly what is open. Walk-ins are welcome too. Call (619) 825-9989.

Are walk-ins available?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome any day during open hours. The fastest way to know if a room is free right now is to call. If we are booked, we will tell you when the next opening is.

What massage should I ask for?

If you are not sure, customized is the easiest first visit. If one specific spot hurts, ask for deep tissue. If you just want to unwind, ask for Swedish or relaxation. Or call and we will help you choose.

Can I request lighter or stronger pressure?

Always. Tell us at the start of the session and any time during. Some people like deep, slow pressure. Others want light and gentle. There is no wrong answer. Speak up and we will match it.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is not posted online and varies by service and length. Please call (619) 825-9989 for the current rate.

Is tipping expected?

Tipping is appreciated but never required.
