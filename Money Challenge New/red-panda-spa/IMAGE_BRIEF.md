# Fletcher Massage — Image Brief

The site uses spa-interior detail photography that fits the warm
ivory + brown + deep red palette. Every image is massage-related.

To replace any image, paste the prompt below into ChatGPT image
generation or Midjourney, download the result, and overwrite the
matching file at `images/<filename>`.

## Global rules for every prompt

- Photorealistic editorial quality, 35mm look, soft natural light
- Warm palette: ivory `#FBF6EC`, espresso brown `#2A1B12`, warm wood,
  deep persimmon `#B23A2A` accent
- Soft amber lantern lighting, gentle warm glow
- No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people unless the image is
  specifically a tasteful staff detail
- No sexualized imagery, no glamour-model framing
- Believable as a small La Mesa neighborhood massage spa, not a luxury
  resort
- Clean, tidy, cared-for feeling

## Sample prompts

### `atmosphere-warm.jpg` (hero card + OG)

> "Close, magazine-style detail of a warm massage spa setup: a stack
> of folded ivory linen towels on warm walnut wood, beside a small
> ceramic bowl of warm oil, with a single dark river stone resting
> on the towels. A soft amber lantern glow bathes the scene from the
> upper right. Subtle deep persimmon red textile in the background,
> slightly out of focus. Photorealistic, soft natural light,
> editorial calm stillness. No people."

### `treatment-room.jpg`

> "Quiet, clean massage treatment room. A single massage table
> dressed in fresh ivory linen, centered in the frame. Warm wood
> floors and walls, a small plant in the corner. Soft amber lantern
> light falls warmly on the table. A folded towel and a stoneware
> oil bowl on a low wooden stool. Photorealistic, calm and tidy,
> believable small local spa. No people."
