First-time visitor FAQ

A few small things to help your first hour.

Should I call before visiting?
A quick call helps. Tell us what you would like (feet, body, deep tissue, hot stone, Thai, or a combo) and how long, and we will let you know what is open right now and hold a room for you. Call (619) 589-8888.
Are walk-ins welcome?
Yes. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a table is open. Weekday evenings and weekend afternoons are our busier windows, so calling ahead is the easiest way to skip a wait.
What massage should I ask for?
Sore in your shoulders or back? Ask for a body massage or deep tissue. Feet tired? Ask for foot reflexology. Cold tension day? Try hot stone. Active body? Try Thai. Want a little of both feet and body? The combo covers it. Not sure? Call and tell us how the day has been.
Can I request lighter or stronger pressure?
Always, and please do. We will ask at the start, and you can adjust mid-session. Lighter, medium, or firmer is enough. If anything is too much, just say so.
How long are sessions, and what does it cost?
Sessions in private rooms run $30 for 30 minutes, $35 for 45 minutes, $40 for 60 minutes, or $60 for 90 minutes. The combo (body and foot) is $25 for 60 minutes or $40 for 90 minutes. Cash, credit card, and Apple Pay all accepted. Birthday $10 off when it is your birthday week. Tipping is appreciated but never required.
Where exactly is Fletcher Massage?
We are at 8182 Parkway Dr, La Mesa, CA 91942, on Fletcher Parkway, next to Chick-fil-A and across from the Costco gas station. Open directions in Google Maps.
How do I get directions from where I am?
Tap to open Google Maps. It will route you from wherever you are. We are easy to find on Fletcher Parkway, just look for the storefront sign.
