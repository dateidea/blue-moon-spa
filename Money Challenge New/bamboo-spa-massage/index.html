What to expect

Six small steps
from the door to the deep breath.

  1. 1

    Call or stop by

    (619) 505-9898 is the fastest way to confirm a room and the current price. Walk ins are welcome any open hour, with availability based on demand.

  2. 2

    Ask what is open today

    The front desk will tell you what is available right now and how long you have. No pressure to decide before you arrive.

  3. 3

    Choose your session

    Bamboo, body, foot, hot stone, deep tissue, or Thai. Pick the time, 30, 60, or 90 minutes, and we will pair you with a therapist.

  4. 4

    Share your pressure preference

    Light, medium, or firm. If you don't know, start with medium. Speak up in the first ten minutes if it needs adjusting.

  5. 5

    Settle into the room

    Body work happens on a table in a private room with the door closed. Foot work happens in a heated recliner. Both formats are one person at a time.

  6. 6

    Leave a little lighter

    Drink some water, walk slow for a minute. Cash, card, or Apple Pay at the front desk. Tip is appreciated, never required.
