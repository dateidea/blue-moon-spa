# Image generation prompts — Kim Spa massage

Use these if you want to replace the four placeholder photos in `/images/`. They are written for a model like DALL·E, Midjourney, or Imagen.

The visual brief: **honest, modest, slightly imperfect. Not a luxury spa. Not a resort.** This is a small storefront massage shop on a busy street in San Diego — the photos should reflect that, not upgrade it.

---

## 1. `storefront.jpg` — exterior

> A small neighborhood massage shop storefront on a sunny weekday in San Diego. Single-story commercial building on College Ave. Modest signage that reads "Kim Spa massage" in simple lettering. Clean glass door, a small neon "Open" sign in the window, a couple of potted plants near the entrance. Mid-afternoon natural light, warm sidewalk, a parked car partially visible at the edge. Realistic photo, slight haze, not staged. 4:3 aspect ratio. No people. No exaggerated luxury elements.

---

## 2. `treatment-room.jpg` — interior treatment room

> A small, simple massage treatment room in a neighborhood spa. One single massage table draped with a clean white sheet and a folded towel. Soft warm lamp on a side table. A bottle of massage oil and a small ceramic cup. Walls a muted warm beige. A small painting on the wall. No people. Realistic photo, daylight diffused through a curtain, not over-styled. 4:3 aspect ratio. Modest, real-world scale, not a luxury suite.

---

## 3. `reception.jpg` — front desk / reception

> The small reception area of a neighborhood massage shop. A modest wooden front desk, a small lamp, a price card propped up, a card reader, and a stack of business cards. A potted plant in the corner. Two simple chairs along the wall for waiting. Warm soft lighting, slightly worn but clean and inviting. No people. Realistic photo, 4:3 aspect ratio. Not a fancy hotel lobby — a small local business.

---

## 4. `quiet-corner.jpg` — interior detail

> A quiet corner of a small massage spa. A single wooden chair with a folded towel and slippers placed neatly on top. A small side table with a candle (unlit), a glass of water, and a tiny ceramic dish. Soft warm light, a sliver of a window with a sheer curtain. No people. Realistic photo, intimate and grounded, slight imperfection. 4:3 aspect ratio.

---

## Negative prompts (use across all four)

`luxury resort, marble, infinity pool, chandelier, gold accents, models, sexualized imagery, magazine cover styling, glossy plastic surfaces, flower petals on bed, candles in fancy formation, fish-eye lens, HDR over-saturation, neon glow.`
