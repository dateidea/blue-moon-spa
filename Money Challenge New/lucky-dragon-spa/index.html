First time visitor

Quick answers before you call.

Anything not covered here? Just give us a call. We are happy to help you pick.

Should I call before visiting?
Yes. A quick call to (619) 740 1232 is the easiest way to confirm a time and avoid waiting. We will tell you what slots are open today.
What are your hours?
Open every day from 9 AM to 11 PM. The hours panel above also highlights today’s row.
What kind of massage should I choose?
If you are new, start with a 60 minute full body massage at medium pressure. Achy from a workout or a long drive? Try deep tissue. Cold and stiff? Hot stone is the move. Stressed and racing? Aromatherapy with calming oils. Still unsure? Tell us how you feel and we will suggest.
Can I request lighter or stronger pressure?
Absolutely. And you can change your mind any time during the session. Your therapist will check in, and a quick “a little lighter please” or “go a bit deeper here” is exactly what we want to hear.
How long are sessions, and what do they cost?
Sessions are 30, 60, or 90 minutes. Pricing can change, so please call (619) 740 1232 for today’s rates and any add ons.
Where exactly is Lucky Dragon Spa?
We are at 5269 Baltimore Dr, La Mesa, CA 91942. In La Mesa, just east of Interstate 8. Get directions or open in Google Maps.
Do you accept cards?
Yes. Major credit cards including Visa and Mastercard. Cash works too, and tips for your therapist are appreciated.
Is the studio accessible?
Yes. Wheelchair accessible entrance, with gender neutral restrooms. If you have a specific access need, mention it on the phone and we will have everything ready.

Ready to feel better today?

Pick up the phone and we will meet you where you are. Sore, stressed, or just ready for a quiet hour.
