Common questions

Everything that's asked at the desk.

If something below isn't confirmed publicly, the answer says so honestly — call the spa to check.

Is the rate really fifty dollars for a full hour?

Yes — the publicly listed rate is $50 for a 60-minute massage. Anything beyond that (longer sessions, focused add-ons) is arranged when you call.

Are walk-ins okay, or do I need an appointment?

The public listing doesn't formally confirm a walk-in policy either way. The honest move is a quick phone call before you head over — most days slots open up within fifteen minutes if the spa isn't full.

How late can I really come in?

The listed close is 11 PM. To start a full sixty-minute session before close, plan to arrive by 9:45 PM. If you're cutting it tight, call from the car so they can hold the room.

Where do I park?

Street parking on University Avenue and the side streets immediately around 6178 is the standard option. Specific lot details aren't listed publicly — call the spa for the easiest spot to aim for at the time you're coming.

What modalities does the therapist offer — Swedish, deep tissue, foot reflexology?

The public listing names a single 60-minute massage rather than a modality menu. Pressure, focus areas, and any specific style are best worked out with the therapist when you call or arrive. Don't expect a printed list.

Do you take cards or just cash?

Payment methods aren't confirmed in the public listing. The safe answer: bring some cash, and call ahead to confirm card acceptance for the day you're going.
