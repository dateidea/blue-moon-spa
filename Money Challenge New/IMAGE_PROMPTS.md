# Oasis 8 Massage — Image Prompts

These prompts produce the on-brand "botanical water retreat" imagery for the
site. Use them with ChatGPT image generation (or any high-quality
text-to-image model). The goal is **photoreal, calm, believable local massage
spa** — *not* luxury resort, *not* tropical vacation, *not* generic stock.

Shared style spine for every prompt:

> Photoreal editorial photo, natural soft warm lighting through linen curtains,
> warm sand and ivory color palette, deep jade green accent details, muted aqua
> ceramic accents, natural stone textures, subtle plant details (small fern, a
> single eucalyptus stem, no oversized tropicals). Calm, refreshing, peaceful
> mood. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people unless specified, shallow
> depth of field, 35mm, ISO 200, soft warm grade.

---

## 1. Hero (`images/hero.jpg`)

> A small, calm massage room photographed at eye level. Clean massage table
> dressed with neatly folded warm-ivory towels and a soft sand-colored throw.
> Warm afternoon light filtering through gauzy linen curtains. A muted aqua
> ceramic bowl with a single sprig of eucalyptus on a side table. Warm wood
> floor, ivory walls with one deep jade green accent panel. Low ceramic stone
> next to a soft beeswax candle. Believable local spa room — not luxurious,
> just clean and quiet. Photoreal, editorial, 35mm, natural light.

## 2. Foot soak (`images/foot-soak.jpg`)

> A handcrafted wooden foot-soak basin filled with warm water and a small
> mound of Epsom salts dissolving on the surface. Steam rising softly. Folded
> ivory towel on the floor next to it. Small jade-green ceramic dish with
> dried botanicals. Warm hardwood floor, sandy linen rug. Macro detail, soft
> warm light, peaceful, calming. No human, no logos.

## 3. Hot stones (`images/hot-stones.jpg`)

> Top-down close-up of smooth basalt stones arranged on a natural wooden tray,
> still warm and faintly glistening. A single fresh eucalyptus leaf to the
> side, a folded ivory cotton towel underneath. Warm light from one side. Tiny
> tendrils of steam. Natural textures: linen, wood, stone. Photoreal, editorial.

## 4. Oil lamp / quiet detail (`images/oil-lamp.jpg`)

> Quiet still life: a small ceramic oil lamp with a soft amber flame on a
> warm-toned wood side table. Folded ivory towel. A muted aqua glass bottle of
> massage oil. A single stem of fern in a tiny pottery vase. Warm window light
> just out of frame. Calm, intimate, local-spa mood. Photoreal.

## 5. Couples / cedar room (`images/cedar-room.jpg`)

> A small, private massage room with two clean massage tables arranged side by
> side, each dressed with neatly folded warm-ivory towels. Walls in soft sand
> with a single deep jade-green accent. Two small ceramic stone bowls between
> the tables holding eucalyptus sprigs. Soft late-afternoon sun coming through
> linen curtains. Calm, simple, believable for a small local spa. No people.

## 6. Treatment room interior (`images/room-warm.jpg`)

> Wide-angle interior of a single calm treatment room. One massage table
> centered, made up with ivory linens and a warm sand throw, soft pillow under
> the headrest cradle. Warm wood floor, soft sand walls, a single deep jade
> green textile on the wall (no text, no patterns). One small leafy plant in a
> stoneware pot in the corner. Warm window light. Photoreal, editorial, calm.

## Optional staff portrait (use **at most one**)

> Editorial half-portrait of a calm, professional massage therapist standing
> in a softly lit treatment room. Adult woman, age 30s, modest neutral spa
> uniform (ivory tunic, no logos, no jewelry visible), natural minimal makeup,
> hair tied back, gentle welcoming smile, hands relaxed in front. Soft
> diffused window light. The image must read as *trustworthy and competent*,
> never sexualized. No revealing clothing, no suggestive pose, no contact with
> a body. Photoreal, editorial, 50mm, shallow depth of field.

> If using this image: keep it small in the layout, frame it as part of a
> "Your therapist" trust block. Do not stack multiple staff portraits — that
> reads as sketchy. One quiet image is enough.

---

## What to avoid in every prompt

- No oily skin close-ups, no suggestive massage poses, no draped bodies on tables.
- No tropical resort imagery (palm trees, ocean, infinity pools).
- No "neon Asian massage" lighting (saturated blue/purple).
- No medical imagery (latex gloves, stainless steel, clinical white).
- No giant exotic flowers (orchid stacks, lotus close-ups).
- No fake awards, badges, "5-star spa" overlays.
- No logos, no text in images, no watermarks.
- No AI plastic-skin faces. If a face appears, it must read as a real person.
