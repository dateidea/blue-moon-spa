No. 08 · First-time questions

A few things
we get asked a lot.

How do I book a massage?

Call (626) 522-2888 any time from 9am to 11pm. We will tell you what is available, confirm the rate, and hold a quiet room for you.

What massage should I ask for?

If you are sore, deep tissue or a body massage. If you want to relax, Swedish or hot stone. If you want the full reset, the 1 hour custom body massage covers Swedish, deep tissue, or a combination, with hot stone and essential oil included. If you are unsure, call and we will suggest a style.

Can I request lighter or stronger pressure?

Always. Tell us your preference at the start of the session, light, medium, or firm, and speak up at any point if you want us to adjust. We would rather know.

Where is Wellness located?

4539 College Ave, San Diego, CA 92115. We are between the College Area and Rolando. Plus code QX93+MH. Easy parking on the lot in front.

How do I get directions?

Tap Get directions to open Google Maps. From most of San Diego it is a short drive on the I-8 or down El Cajon Blvd.

How much does a massage cost?

Our 1 hour custom body massage is $79.99 and includes your choice of Swedish, deep tissue, or a combination, plus essential oil and hot stone at no extra charge. For longer sessions or anything else, call (626) 522-2888 for the current rate.

Is the spa LGBTQ+ friendly?

Yes. Every guest is welcome, every night.
