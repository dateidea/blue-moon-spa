First time visitor FAQ

A few quick answers before you stop in.

Should I call before visiting?

Yes. A quick call is the easiest way to confirm the next open slot. Phone: (314) 452-2322.

How do I book?

Call (314) 452-2322 and we will let you know what time slots are open today. Online booking is also available on the official Liberty Bodyworks website.

What massage should I ask for?

For tight shoulders or back tension, ask for full body with hot stones or deep tissue. For tired feet, the foot massage with the warm Epsom soak is the easy pick. If you are unsure, just describe what feels off and we will suggest something.

Can I request lighter or stronger pressure?

Of course. Tell your therapist your preference at the start, and ask for adjustments any time during the session.

Where is Liberty Bodyworks located?

7900 El Cajon Blvd, Suite C, San Diego, CA 92115. Front parking is available, and we are a short walk from SDSU.

How do I get directions?

Use the Google Maps directions link or tap Open in Google Maps.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. 10% off all services for active duty and veterans with a valid military ID.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash and major credit cards. Please call if you have a specific question.
